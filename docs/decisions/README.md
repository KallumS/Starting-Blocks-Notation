# Decisions

One file per decision that would otherwise have to be re-argued from scratch,
in the usual shape: what the situation was, what was chosen, and what that
costs. They are dated and numbered and **they are not edited to stay true** -
if a decision is reversed, the old record keeps its text and gains a line at
the top pointing at the one that replaced it. A record that quietly changed
its mind is worth nothing.

What belongs here: a choice someone could reasonably make the other way, where
the reasoning is not obvious from the code. What does not: anything the code
already says plainly, and anything that was never really a choice.

`CLAUDE.md` is the working guide - what to do, and what not to break. These are
the reasons underneath it. Where the two overlap, `CLAUDE.md` is the shorter
version and points here.
